CONSTITUTIONAL MAINTENANCE PROGRAM
Constitutional Maintenance Inquiry Worksheet
Reusable research tool  ·  Independent use 
Use a copy for one specific governmental question, including a safeguard that appears to work. Complete only the parts that help establish what is known and what action, if any, is justified; mark unknowns rather than guessing. A useful first pass may require only a clear question, a primary source, relevant contrary evidence, and a possible next action. Complete additional sections as the inquiry develops. This is a reusable, independent research tool—not a submission or approval process. Do not include sensitive personal records or confidential government or Tribal information in public copies.

If a change is worth exploring (optional)
•  Compare possible responses, including existing-law remedies, administrative action, further oversight, no action, and possible legislation:
•  If a particular response is proposed: what legal authority and responsible institution would carry it out?
•  What would implementation require (people, systems, funding, time, other offices, and necessary handoffs)?
•  Whose rights or interests are affected; what safeguards, review, appeals, and outcome checks would be needed?
•  What happens if a necessary step fails or an institution changes? Identify rights and duties that survive:
•  What fiscal, legal, factual, operational, or formal questions require further examination? Record contrary analysis:

What action, if any, should follow?
•  Working assessment: functioning safeguard / lawful outcome / possible problem / corrected under existing means / unresolved / no substantiated failure:
•  Action supported now, if any: none / obtain records / oversight question / existing remedy / administrative correction / referral / explore legislation / other:
•  Why does the current evidence support that action rather than a stronger or different one?
•  Who has lawful authority to act, by what route, and what is the next concrete step?
•  If action is premature, what specific evidence or legal decision is needed first?
•  What result would show the issue resolved—or require the assessment to be revised?
•  Dated corrections, source/attribution notes, and sharing or confidentiality limits:
A valid outcome can be a working safeguard, an unresolved question, an existing-law resolution, or no new law. Do not turn an open question into an established failure or assume every inquiry must produce legislation. Independent use needs no approval.
